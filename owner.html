    Welcome — setup checklist

    Follow these steps in order to launch your storefront. Most merchants finish setup in under 15 minutes.

    • 1. Business & Brand
      Settings → Business and Brand to set name, currency, tax, brand colors, and logo.
    • 2. Add your products
      Open Products → Add product. Upload clean product images to Cloudflare R2 (max 10 MB each).
    • 3. Payments & WhatsApp
      Settings → Payment / Manual Payment + WhatsApp Numbers. Enable Paystack & Flutterwave, then verify webhook signatures in your provider dashboards.
    • 4. Storefront polish
      Fill Features, Specifications, FAQ, Testimonials, SEO, Trust Badges and Sticky CTA before driving traffic.
    • 5. Live smoke test
      Place a Flutterwave / Paystack test purchase and a Manual bank order. Open Orders & Lookup to confirm the reference appears here.
